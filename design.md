Design System & Palette (Cort Edition)
Typography: Inter or Roboto (Clean, Sans Serif). High legibility.

Primary Backgrounds: White or Light Grey Tint (#F5F5F5).

Core Color Palette:

Corporate Anchor (Headers/Nav): Deep Navy (#0c225e). Used for top bars, sidebars, and primary text.

Primary Action (CTAs): Cort Orange (#f47f00). Used for "Book Now," "Submit," "Confirm," and "Accept."

Secondary/State: Royal Purple (#670e4c). Used for selected states (active tabs), toggles, or highlighting "Special/VIP" elements.

Info/Accents: Cort Blue (#09347d) or Teal (#6db9ca). Used for information badges or non-critical icons.

Critical Safety: Standard Red (#D32F2F) and Traffic Green (#388E3C). Note: These override brand colors only for "Start/Stop Driving" buttons.
